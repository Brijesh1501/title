LinkedIn Verification

For each contact with a LinkedIn URL, checks whether their profile currently shows them at the company already on file for them (their Account Name) — and captures their current title when it does. Uses Bright Data's LinkedIn Profiles dataset via this project's own server-side proxy, so no API key ever touches the browser.

This calls a paid, external API. Every unique LinkedIn URL submitted is billed by Bright Data against this project's account, and a job can take anywhere from under a minute to several minutes to finish — you can leave this tab open and it'll keep checking automatically.

Scraping profile data from LinkedIn also sits in a legal and terms-of-service gray area; this tool doesn't make that determination for you — confirm your organization's use of Bright Data's LinkedIn dataset is something you're comfortable with before running it.
